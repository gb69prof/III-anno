# Fonti scientifiche

## Fonti primarie ed edizioni

1. Peter Abelard, Historia Calamitatum, Internet Medieval Sourcebook, Fordham University: https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/basis/abelard-histcal.asp
2. Heloise, Letter to Abelard, Internet Medieval Sourcebook, Fordham University: https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/source/heloise1.asp
3. Heloise, Letter 6, testo latino e traduzione, Epistolæ, Columbia University Libraries: https://epistolae.ctl.columbia.edu/letter/902.html
4. David Luscombe (ed.), The Letter Collection of Peter Abelard and Heloise, Oxford University Press, 2013.

## Studi

5. Peter King, “Peter Abelard”, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/abelard/
6. John Marenbon, “The letters of Abelard and Heloise”, in The Philosophy of Peter Abelard, Cambridge University Press.
7. Constant J. Mews, “Heloise and the Paraclete”, in Abelard and Heloise, Oxford University Press.
8. Jocelyn Wogan-Browne e Vera Morton, “Abelard to Heloise: The History of Women’s Roles in Christianity”, Cambridge Core.
9. Andrea Nye, “A Woman’s Thought or a Man’s Discipline?”, Hypatia.
10. Jane Duran, “Heloise: A Christian View on Ethics and Love”, New Blackfriars.
11. Julia Barrow, Charles Burnett, David Luscombe, “A checklist of the manuscripts containing the writings of Peter Abelard and Heloise”, Revue d’histoire des textes.
12. Sarah Spence, “Text of the body: Abelard and Guibert de Nogent”, in Texts and the Self in the Twelfth Century, Cambridge University Press.

Le traduzioni italiane brevi e le parafrasi didattiche sono originali e non sostituiscono un’edizione critica.
